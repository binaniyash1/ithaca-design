# Field-service neutral benchmark — verified feedback regression 1

Source: comparison of the agent-generated Fieldline surface with Yash's manual
interpretation, verified 31 August 2026.

- Neutral canvas and white focal surfaces are retained; the correction is
  internal structure, not stronger canvas contrast.
- Multiple rule-generated work groups are visually segregated into
  understandable decision units instead of merging into one overwhelming
  surface.
- When comparable work groups coexist, spacing is larger between groups and
  compact and regular inside their repeated rows.
- When rows contain many heterogeneous variables, visible column labels or an
  equally clear aid explains their schema. Simple rows are not forced to have
  headers.
- Metric count is not prescribed. If metrics use individual cards, every card
  has matched white surface, quiet border, consistent padding and radius,
  subdued label, differentiated mono value, and restrained semantic support.
- The contextual right pane remains part of the interaction but is closed by
  default when its content depends on selection. Clicking a row opens that
  row's context.
- Ambient content may occupy the contextual pane only when it materially
  changes a decision before selection; it is not filler for available width.
- Realistic domain data, deadline semantics, technician assignment, tailored
  row verbs, stable action placement, and distinct urgency states are retained.
